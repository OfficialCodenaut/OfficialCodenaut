# Hey, I'm Codenaut 👋

**"One bug away from reentry." 🚀**

I’m a developer from Poland focused on building, understanding, and breaking systems — just to rebuild them better.

Currently a technical high school student learning software development, but most of my real progress comes from outside the classroom.

I’m especially interested in how systems behave under pressure, how they fail, and how they can be made more resilient.

---

### 🛰️ Lore behind my nickname

"Codenaut" is a mix of *code* and *astronaut* — not as a space industry ambition anymore, but as a way of thinking.

I treat software like exploration:

- you enter unknown systems  
- you map what’s inside  
- you break things to understand them  
- and you learn how to survive the environment  

Same curiosity, different universe.
